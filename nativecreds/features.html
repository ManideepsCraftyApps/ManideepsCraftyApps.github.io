Safe by default

Locks when you leave

Press home, switch apps or turn the screen off and the vault locks.

Clipboard cleans itself

Copied passwords and codes clear after 30 seconds, even in another app.

Hidden from screenshots

Screenshots, screen recording and the recent-apps preview are blocked.

What it does not do yet

  • Passkeys, QR scanning for 2FA, and changing the master password inside the app.
  • Versions for iPhone, Mac or Windows. Android only for now.
  • Nobody can reset a forgotten master password, not even me. Keep a backup copy of your vault file.
