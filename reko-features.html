What Reko does

You copy things all day — text, screenshots, files, whole paragraphs from three different tabs. Reko remembers every one of them, so you never have to go find where it went.

Nothing you copy just disappears

You copy something, then copy something else, and the first thing is gone — that's how the Mac clipboard has always worked. Reko sits quietly in your menu bar and keeps a running history of everything you copy, exactly as you copied it: plain text, rich formatting, images, files, whatever. Copy ten things in a row and all ten are still there when you need them.

Screenshot needed The quick window open, showing a history list with a mix of text, an image, and a file item

One shortcut, from anywhere

Press your shortcut while you're typing in any app, and your clipboard history pops up right there — no switching windows, no hunting through a menu bar icon. Click the item you want and it's pasted. Click away, or hit Escape, and it's gone like it was never there.

Screenshot needed The quick window open on top of another app (e.g. Mail or Notes), mid-paste

Paste it your way

Sometimes you want the formatting that came with what you copied. Sometimes you just want the plain text, no font sizes or colors fighting with whatever you're pasting into. Reko lets you choose per-paste — clean when you want it clean, exact when you want it exact — and can auto-paste the moment you click, so there's no extra ⌘V after.

Fill in a whole form without touching the mouse

Copied five things you need to paste into five different fields, one after another? Reko's sequential paste steps through your selection in order, one press of a shortcut per field — no re-opening the history window each time, no re-selecting.

Keep things sorted your way

Group items into categories that make sense to you — work, code snippets, addresses, whatever fits how you actually use your clipboard. Each category can even keep its history for a different length of time, so a quick scratch category can clear itself out while the one you rely on daily sticks around.

Screenshot needed Settings → Categories tab, showing a few named categories with colors and retention settings

Some things deserve to stay private

Not everything you copy is meant to sit around in plain view — a password, a private note, something sensitive. Mark an item Private and it's encrypted on disk and hidden behind Face ID, Touch ID, or your Mac password. Unlock once and everything marked private stays visible while you're actively working, then locks itself back down.

Screenshot needed Two states side by side (or a GIF-style pair): a private item masked/locked, and the same item revealed after Touch ID

Find the one you're thinking of

A history is only useful if you can actually find what you want in it. Search across everything you've copied, or narrow it down by category, which app it came from, what kind of content it is, when you copied it — even filter down to just your private items.

Screenshot needed The quick window with the search bar and filter chips visible, mid-search

Turn a handful of copies into one clean result

Select several items you've copied and merge them into one — either as a single new clipboard item ready to paste, or as a real, properly formatted .docx you can open straight in Pages or Word. Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, fonts, sizes, colors — all the formatting from each original piece carries over, and files you selected are included by name so nothing gets lost in the merge.

  • Merge — combines your selection into one pasteable item, in order
  • Merge as Document — builds an actual Word document with full formatting retained
Screenshot needed A merged .docx opened in Pages/Word, showing retained bold/italic/color/font formatting from different sources

Text inside a screenshot is still text

Copy or capture an image with text in it — a screenshot of an error message, a photo of a whiteboard — and Reko can pull the actual text out of it on-device, ready to search or paste, without ever sending the image anywhere.

Barcodes and QR codes, read automatically

If an image you copy contains a barcode or QR code, Reko detects it and decodes it for you, so you don't need a separate scanner app for something already sitting in your clipboard history.

Screenshot needed An image item in the history showing the OCR-extracted text and/or a decoded barcode/QR value

Your history, backed up on your terms

Export your entire clipboard history to a single encrypted file, protected by a password only you know, and bring it back on another Mac (or the same one, after a fresh install) whenever you want. Nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly export it.
