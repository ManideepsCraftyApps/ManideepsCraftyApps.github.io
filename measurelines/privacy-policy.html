Privacy policy

Measure Lines Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

Measure Lines is an on-screen ruler for Android, made by Manideep's Crafty Apps (“I”, “me”). Measure Lines does not collect, transmit or share any of your data. I never receive anything from the app. There is no account, no server, no ads and no analytics, and the app does not even have Android's internet permission.

No account, no servers, no internet access

Measure Lines has no user accounts and I do not run any server for it. The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot open network connections. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking code.

What it does

Measure Lines draws rulers on your screen, either inside the app or on top of other apps, so you can measure things you see on the display. It measures only the screen itself. It does not read, capture or record the content of other apps or of your screen.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Display over other apps: only to draw the ruler on top of other apps, which is the app's core feature. You grant it yourself in Android's settings and can withdraw it at any time.
  • Foreground service (special use): only to keep the ruler overlay running reliably while you have it turned on.
  • Notifications: Android requires a notification while that service is running. It only shows the app's own status and shortcuts (open the app, lock or hide the ruler). It never contains ads or third-party content.
  • No internet, storage, contacts, location, camera, microphone, accessibility or notification-access permission is requested.

What Measure Lines stores on your device

The app saves a few settings in its private app storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere by the app.

  • Preferences: your measurement unit, ruler colour theme and opacity, app theme (system, light or dark), whether the cursor, crosshair, angle readout and angle snapping are on, and your custom scale (a number and a short label such as “m”).
  • Calibration: a single number (pixels per centimetre) if you calibrate the ruler against a real object.
  • State: whether the ruler overlay is currently on or locked.

The ruler's position, size and rotation are not saved. Measure Lines opts out of Android's automatic backup, so these settings are not copied to your Google account or to a new device.

Clipboard

When you tap a cursor reading or the area readout, that one measurement (for example “3.42 cm”) is copied to the clipboard. Nothing is copied unless you tap. The app never reads what is on your clipboard. While something is on the clipboard, other software on your device, for example your keyboard or a clipboard-sync feature, may be able to read it.

Data sharing

I do not share your data with anyone, because I never have it.

Retention and deletion

Everything listed above stays on your device until you remove it. Clearing Measure Lines' storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no account and no server-side data to delete.

Children

Measure Lines is a general-purpose tool not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, this policy will be updated and the effective date changed.
