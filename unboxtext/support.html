What Unbox Text can do

  • Instant on-device text recognition from any region of your screen
  • Searchable, editable capture history — nothing to retype twice
  • Capture Viewer — a three-pane window to review the original image, edit the recognized text, and see confidence-highlighted words
  • PDF and image import, including page-range selection for multi-page PDFs
  • Finder "Open With" integration for PDFs and images
  • Optional Markdown-formatted output for Obsidian, Notion, and similar apps
  • Text-to-speech read-aloud with a floating, always-on-top control
  • Automatic QR code and barcode detection
  • Multi-language automatic detection
  • Optional "Handwriting Expert" cloud OCR for messy or cursive handwriting, using your own Azure account
  • Fully private by default — no accounts, no ads, no tracking, nothing leaves your Mac unless you choose to enable Handwriting Expert
